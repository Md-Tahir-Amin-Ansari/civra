Welcome to Civra

Private, offline AI chat that runs entirely on your computer.

Before you start, Civra needs to download the AI model to your computer (approximately 2.4 GB).
An internet connection is needed only for this one-time download. After that, all conversations happen locally on your device.
Your chats never leave your computer. No account, no cloud, no tracking.
Please note: AI-generated answers can be inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading. Do not rely on them for medical, legal, financial, or other important decisions. Running the model locally reduces some privacy risks, but it does not guarantee absolute security.
